About Rescue Bite

Good food, better purpose.

Rescue Bite connects restaurants with students and neighbours looking for affordable meals. Instead of sending safe, unsold food to waste, restaurants can share it near closing time and recover value.

Every reservation supports a more affordable food culture and keeps edible meals in the community.
